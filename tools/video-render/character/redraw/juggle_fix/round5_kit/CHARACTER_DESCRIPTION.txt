THE WHITE KEY: A DETAILED DESCRIPTION
(measured on the 28 reference frames; "card width" and "card height" mean the cream face of the card)

Overall
A small, cute cartoon character: a white piano key drawn as a thick cream card standing upright on two
short legs, wearing a black beret. Soft, friendly, childlike. Flat colours, one clean black outline, no
gradients, no textures, no shading except a pale rim inside the card's outline. He is drawn slightly in
three-quarter view: we see the front of the card and a thin slice of its left side.

Colours (exact)
- Card face: warm cream #FBDEBB
- Pale rim just inside the card's outline: off-white #FCF9EA
- Left side of the card: lilac #9E9BFA
- Cheeks: orange #FC9A47
- Beret, outline, eyes, mouth, arms, legs, shoes: black #000000
- Hands: the same cream as the card, with a black outline
- Three tiny freckle dots on the lower half of the card: a slightly darker orange-cream

The card (his body)
- A rectangle with generously rounded corners, 1.45 times as tall as it is wide (never slimmer than 1.4,
  never squarer than 1.5 when he stands upright).
- It is not a flat front-on rectangle: it leans a few degrees (the top a little to the right of the
  bottom) and its top and bottom edges slope slightly, so it reads as a thick card seen a little from the
  left. In jumps and leans the whole card tilts up to about 10 degrees; it never bends or squashes.
- Outline: one even black line, about 1/40 of the card width thick (about 5 px when the card is 256 px
  tall). The same thickness everywhere on the character.
- Just inside the outline runs a thin off-white rim, about as wide as the outline, all the way round the
  face of the card.
- The left side: a lilac strip down the whole left edge, about 1/16 of the card width wide, with its own
  black outline on the outside. It has rounded ends that follow the card's corners. Nothing is on the
  right side.

The beret
- Solid black, a flattened dome like a small cushion, a little wider than the card (about 1.1 times the
  card width) and about 1/4 of the card height tall.
- It sits ON the top edge of the card and slightly behind it: the card's top edge and rim overlap the
  bottom of the beret. It is shifted a little to the left, so it overhangs the lilac side.
- A short curved stalk on top, slightly left of the middle, leaning to the left, about 1/8 of the card
  width long with a rounded end.

The face
- In the upper third of the card, centred.
- Eyes: two solid black round dots, each about 1/10 of the card width across, about 0.4 of the card width
  apart. No whites, no highlights, no lashes, no eyebrows.
- Mouth: a small black "u" smile between and slightly below the eyes, about 1/6 of the card width wide.
  When delighted: a small open mouth, black with a red-pink tongue. 
- Cheeks: two solid orange circles, each about 1/7 of the card width across, just below and outside the
  eyes, touching nothing.
- Closed happy eyes: two small black arcs (like upside-down "u"s) in place of the dots.

The arms and hands
- Arms: plain black lines, about as thick as two outlines (about 1/18 of the card width), coming out of
  the card's sides about halfway down. No elbows drawn; they bend in a soft curve. Each arm is about 0.45
  of the card height long.
- Hands: small cream mittens with a black outline, about 1/6 of the card width across: a rounded palm
  with three short rounded finger bumps and a thumb bump. No gloves, no cuffs.

The legs and shoes
- Two black legs coming straight out of the bottom edge of the card, about 0.29 of the card height long
  and about 1/8 of the card width thick, set about 1/4 of the card width in from each side. They are
  soft tubes, usually very slightly bent, not rigid sticks.
- Shoes: each leg ends in a small rounded black shoe pointing outward and slightly forward, a soft bean
  shape about twice as wide as the leg. Not a round ball, not a flat bar.

Size on the output sheet (each cell 768 x 512)
- Card face about 256 px tall and about 177 px wide, centred near x 384.
- Shoes' bottom on the ground line at y 496.
- With the beret he is about 400 px tall, from about y 96 to y 496.

How he moves (from the reference frames)
- Standing: arms hanging slightly out, hands low. Waving: one arm up with small curved motion lines.
- Jumping: the card lifts, legs bend up and back, small curved motion lines at the feet.
- Cheering: both arms up and out, hands open.
- He is always the same size and the same shape; only the tilt, the arms, the legs and the face change.
